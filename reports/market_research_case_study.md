# Market research workflow: public portfolio summary

This is a newly written client-free summary, not a redacted facsimile of the original client deck.

## Question and scope
The research connected industry demand, regional distribution, equipment demand and channel coverage. A separate workstream organized marketing-activity planning and reporting. The archive does not disclose a client's sales, market-share targets, outlet performance or interview responses.

## Work products and method
1. Desk-research outline: distinguish industry size, imports, end-use segments, competition and regional clusters.
2. Source register: record publication date, measurement year, currency, unit, geography and whether a figure is observed or forecast.
3. Reconciliation: compare like-for-like definitions; never treat a count of shipments as a count of machines or mix industrial and household equipment totals.
4. Report structure: separate evidence, interpretation and proposed follow-up. Preserve conflicting estimates with explanations rather than selecting the most convenient number.
5. Activity-planning framework: audience, objective, channel, timing, cost category and measurement definition, without client budgets or commitments.

## Reproduction
The synthetic Python/SQL exercise demonstrates how duplicate dimension rows can multiply totals, how aggregation controls the grain, and how unmatched entities are flagged. It is a later learning implementation, not evidence of historical automation or a quantified saving.
